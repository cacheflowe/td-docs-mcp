# Bluefish444

TouchDesigner supports [Bluefish444](http://bluefish444.com) video input and output devices through the [Video Device In TOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Video_Device_In_TOP "Video Device In TOP") and [Video Device Out TOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Video_Device_Out_TOP "Video Device Out TOP").
[![Bluefish Logo STANDARD.png](https://docs.derivative.ca/images/thumb/8/84/Bluefish_Logo_STANDARD.png/300px-Bluefish_Logo_STANDARD.png)](https://docs.derivative.ca/File:Bluefish_Logo_STANDARD.png)
[![Bluefish444Kronos.jpg](https://docs.derivative.ca/images/thumb/1/15/Bluefish444Kronos.jpg/600px-Bluefish444Kronos.jpg)](https://docs.derivative.ca/File:Bluefish444Kronos.jpg)
The KRONOS range is the latest generation of video, audio, and data processing I/O cards from Bluefish444. Built with multi-channel 4K/UHD 60p, High Dynamic Range, and High Frame-Rate SDI to address applications in broadcast, production, post-production, immersive graphics, live events, streaming and more.

###

Bluefish Support in TouchDesigner

TouchDesigner has built-in native driver support for Bluefish devices.

**Ways to interface with Bluefish444 cards in TouchDesigner**
  * [Video Device In TOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Video_Device_In_TOP "Video Device In TOP") - Captures video streams from capture cards and input devices.

  * [Video Device Out TOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Video_Device_Out_TOP "Video Device Out TOP") - Outputs video.

The term "Frame" is a measurement of time used (1) in the [Timeline](https://docs.derivative.ca/Timeline "Timeline"), (2) as a time-unit in CHOPs, and (3) as a time unit in movie files that are read into [TOPs](https://docs.derivative.ca/TOP "TOP") and written out from TOPs. The frame rate is the frames per second ([FPS](https://docs.derivative.ca/index.php?title=FPS&action=edit&redlink=1 "FPS \(page does not exist\)")).
