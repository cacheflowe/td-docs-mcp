# Connector

The notches on the left and right of each [Node](https://docs.derivative.ca/Node "Node") are Connectors that let you [Wire](https://docs.derivative.ca/Wire "Wire") the output of one [Operator](https://docs.derivative.ca/Operator "Operator") to the input of another operator of the same [Operator Family](https://docs.derivative.ca/Operator "Operator").

The notches on the top and bottom of [3D Object Components](https://docs.derivative.ca/Object "Object") and [Panel Components](https://docs.derivative.ca/Panel_Component "Panel Component") are Connectors that tie the components of each sub-[Family](https://docs.derivative.ca/Operator_Family "Operator Family") in a [Hierarchy](https://docs.derivative.ca/Hierarchy "Hierarchy").

See also [Wire](https://docs.derivative.ca/Wire "Wire"), [Hierarchy](https://docs.derivative.ca/Hierarchy "Hierarchy").
