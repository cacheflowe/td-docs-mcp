# Extend Conditions
In CHOPs, **Extend Conditions** determine what numbers you get when you try to get a channel value that is outside its start-end range - in its **Extend Regions** , before the [CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/CHOP "CHOP")'s start time or after its end time. The CHOP may hold its last value, it may cycle, repeat, or be held at a default value. You can see a CHOP's extend conditions in the info pop-up (middle-mouse click on the CHOP), or by looking at a CHOP in the channel viewer outside its start-end range. You will see that a [Pattern CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Pattern_CHOP "Pattern CHOP") cycles and a [Keyframe CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Keyframe_CHOP "Keyframe CHOP") holds in the Extend Regions.
You can change the Extend Conditions with an [Extend CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Extend_CHOP "Extend CHOP") or via menus on the Channel page in many CHOPs.
