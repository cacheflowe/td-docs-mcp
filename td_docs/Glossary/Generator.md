# Generator
Operators that do not need any inputs connected (to create a meaningful output) are called Generators in TouchDesigner, like a [Pattern CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Pattern_CHOP "Pattern CHOP"). "Inputs" are both the [wires](https://docs.derivative.ca/Wire "Wire") from other operators, and [OP References](https://docs.derivative.ca/Link "Link") to other operators in OP parameters.
Generators are the darker shaded operators in the [OP Create Dialog](https://docs.derivative.ca/OP_Create_Dialog "OP Create Dialog").
See their counterparts [Filters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Filter "Filter").
Operators may have inputs defined by [Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Parameter "Parameter") that are [Path](https://docs.derivative.ca/Network_Path "Network Path") references, that are from any [Operator Family](https://docs.derivative.ca/Operator_Family "Operator Family").
