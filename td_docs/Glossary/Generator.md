# Generator

Operators that do not need any inputs connected (to create a meaningful output) are called Generators in TouchDesigner, like a [Pattern CHOP](../CHOPs/Pattern_CHOP.md "Pattern CHOP"). "Inputs" are both the [wires](Wire.md "Wire") from other operators, and [OP References](Link.md "Link") to other operators in OP parameters.

Generators are the darker shaded operators in the [OP Create Dialog](https://docs.derivative.ca/OP_Create_Dialog "OP Create Dialog").

See their counterparts [Filters](Filter.md "Filter").

Operators may have inputs defined by [Parameters](Parameter.md "Parameter") that are [Path](Network_Path.md "Network Path") references, that are from any [Operator Family](Operator_Family.md "Operator Family").
